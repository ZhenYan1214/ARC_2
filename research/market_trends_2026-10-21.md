## Regulation
- US GENIUS Act effective **2027-01-18** (rules late). OCC proposal: no issuer yield; affiliate/white-label yield presumed violation; merchant discounts OK. P2P/self-custody safe harbours. Freezing/blocking is the norm
- CLARITY Act: Senate cloture failed 2026-09-15 → rewards rules ambiguous until 2027
- EU MiCA: USDT largely delisted since 2026-07-01; Revolut EURR (Bridge); euro stablecoins ~€825M, EURC 60%; EURC on Arc only $8M
- Hong Kong: first licences HSBC & Anchorpoint (Apr 2026), HKDAP institutional only (~$0.8M). Singapore: rules 2027–28. Japan: yen stablecoins ~$155M. Korea: bill not passed
- **Taiwan: Virtual Asset Service Act passed 2026-06-30; stablecoin rules effective Q1 2027 at earliest; no interest** → in 2026 only USD/EUR stablecoins are practical

## Agent's synthesis
Real needs: (1) workflow layer (approvals, conditions, invoice↔payment memos) on top of flows; (2) agent spend governance (budgets, allowlists, refunds) — concrete need, tiny market, matches Circle priority; (3) freeze-aware non-custodial pull/claim design; (4) EURC flows for EU users (niche, open); (5) intra-USDC part of EM cross-border.
Hype for a tiny app now: agent commerce volume, Asian local stablecoins, yield apps, retail FX, stablechain TVL, consumer USDC checkout.
Tempo is Arc's real rival for payroll/enterprise; privacy payroll would lose to Tempo.
